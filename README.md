# 100-Data-Points_V2

The data is about calories of the food items that I have eaten within the last 2 weeks. The food items are represented as asteroids floating around a black hole, the distance between them represents the time taken (in minutes) to finish each food item and the size of it represents the amount calories in the food. There are 25 asteroids that are colour coded in the order of a rainbow "VIBGYOR" where the violet and its shades are the first and earliest food item that I ate within the week and the red is the last food items.
